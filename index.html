HueNotes

Colorful notes and checklists for iPhone and iPad.

  • Bring all your notes over in one go: ColorNote backups from Android (decrypted on your phone), Google Keep, Evernote, Apple Notes, Simplenote and Markdown apps.
  • Text notes and checklists in nine colors, folders, reminders and a calendar.
  • Lock private notes with Face ID and a master password.
  • Widgets, encrypted backups and optional iCloud sync.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no server.
