HueNotes Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Short version: HueNotes does not collect, store or share any of your data. Your notes stay on your devices.

What HueNotes stores

Your notes, checklists, folders, reminders and settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. There is no HueNotes account and no HueNotes server. The developer cannot see your notes.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing, unless you send it. HueNotes contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Backups are encrypted with your backup password and saved where you choose: on the device (included in your device's iCloud backup, which Apple handles), or a folder you pick in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive. Those services are run by their providers under their own privacy policies.
  • iCloud sync (optional, off until you turn it on): your notes, checklists, folders and reminders are stored in your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. Apple runs iCloud under its own privacy policy; only you can read that database, not the developer. Locked notes stay encrypted with your master password. HueNotes also keeps a random salt and a check value there (never the password itself) so locked notes open on your other devices.
  • Imports (ColorNote backups, Google Keep, Evernote, Apple Notes, Markdown and other files) are read and decrypted on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Purchases of HueNotes Pro are handled by Apple. HueNotes only learns whether your Apple ID owns Pro or started the free trial; it never sees your payment details.

Smart features

Search, reminder suggestions, checklists and Tidy up use Apple's on-device language frameworks and, where available, Apple Intelligence on your device. Your text is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only to show the reminders you set.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: only to unlock locked notes and the app. Apple performs the check; HueNotes never receives biometric data.

Children

HueNotes collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
